# Alarm cause / action per code

Alarm_PLC_001_Cause=PLC communication was lost while the recipe was running. The step was aborted and a chamber stop was attempted.

Alarm_PLC_001_Action=Check PLC power and the network cable. Reconnection is retried every 30 seconds. Restart the recipe after the link is restored.

Alarm_PLC_002_Cause=Temperature and humidity could not be read from the PLC for the safety check, so the recipe was stopped.

Alarm_PLC_002_Action=Inspect the chamber directly; it may still be hot. Restart only after PLC communication is restored.

Alarm_PLC_003_Cause=Both chamber stop attempts failed. The heater may still be running.

Alarm_PLC_003_Action=Stop the chamber manually at the PLC panel now. Do not wait for the link to recover.

Alarm_NATS_001_Cause=A command could not be sent to the camera Agent (NATS link down). Only that camera was skipped; the chamber sequence continued.

Alarm_NATS_001_Action=Check the NATS broker and the camera PC network. Capture data for this interval is missing.

Alarm_CAM_001_Cause=The camera Agent did not answer within the timeout.

Alarm_CAM_001_Action=Check that the camera PC is powered on and the Agent is running.

Alarm_CAM_002_Cause=Only some of the requested shots were stored. The rest failed or arrived too late.

Alarm_CAM_002_Action=Check the camera state and free space on the storage path. This step holds partial data only.

Alarm_CAM_003_Cause=A repeat capture round slipped past its interval. The round was skipped instead of firing back-to-back.

Alarm_CAM_003_Action=Increase the interval or reduce the shot count, and investigate why the camera slowed down.

Alarm_BB_001_Cause=Setting or reading the black body temperature failed. The next step ran without reaching the target.

Alarm_BB_001_Action=Check black body power and wiring. Captures in this interval may not match the reference temperature.

Alarm_RCP_001_Cause=The operator stopped the recipe.

Alarm_RCP_001_Action=No action required. The stop point is recorded in the message.

Alarm_RCP_002_Cause=The recipe was aborted by an emergency stop.

Alarm_RCP_002_Action=Clear the cause, release the emergency stop, verify the chamber state, then restart the recipe.

Alarm_RCP_003_Cause=Chamber temperature or humidity left the safety band defined on the step. The recipe waits for operator confirmation.

Alarm_RCP_003_Action=Check the chamber and decide whether to continue. Also verify the safety band values themselves.

Alarm_RCP_004_Cause=The recipe contains a step kind this version does not understand, so it was skipped.

Alarm_RCP_004_Action=Verify that the Master version running matches the one that saved the recipe.
